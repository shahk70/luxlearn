        About

        LuxLearn

        Automatic screen brightness that learns your habits.

        How it works

        LuxLearn starts adjusting your screen brightness right away, using every signal available: the ambient light sensor if your device has one, the webcam or screen content, and a sunrise/sunset curve. Nothing needs to be configured first.

        Your data stays yours

        All learning data is stored locally on this device only. Nothing is ever uploaded. You can export or wipe it at any time from the User Profile page.

        Help

        LuxLearn starts adjusting your screen brightness right away, using whatever it has available: an ambient light sensor if your device has one, your webcam or screen content, and a sunrise/sunset curve for the time of day. There's nothing you need to configure first.

        Every manual brightness change you make is quietly logged and used to fine-tune future predictions. The more it observes, the more it leans on your own habits instead of the generic time-of-day estimate - the "Automation Confidence" figure on the Status page shows how much it currently trusts its own predictions.

        There's no strict on/off "learning phase": adjustments blend the learned model with the safe fallback estimate in proportion to that confidence, so behavior stays sensible with very few logs and keeps improving smoothly as more data comes in. The program keeps learning until it reaches the log limit, and new manual changes continue to refine older, similar logs to keep the data relevant. If you prefer the app to leave brightness untouched until it has learned enough, turn off "Adjust during learning phase" in the settings.
